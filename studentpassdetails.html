Required Documents

  • Student ID: Official student identification from your school or college.
  • Address Proof: Proof of your current address, such as a utility bill or government ID.
  • Passport Size Photos (2): Two recent passport-sized color photographs.

Additional Information

The student monthly pass offers significant discounts for students who commute regularly to their educational institutions. Ensure all documents are valid and up-to-date before applying.
