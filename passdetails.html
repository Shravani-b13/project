Student Monthly Pass Requirements

Required Documents

  • Student ID: Official student identification from your school or college.
  • Address Proof: Proof of your current address, such as a utility bill or government ID.
  • Passport Size Photos (2): Two recent passport-sized color photographs.

Additional Information

The student monthly pass is a special discount pass for students who commute to their educational institutions regularly. To apply, make sure your documents are up-to-date and valid.
