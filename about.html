Why Digital Passes?

In today's fast-paced world, standing in long queues for physical passes can be a hassle. Digital passes offer a convenient solution, saving time, reducing paperwork, and minimizing errors. With PASS SEVA, users can easily access, renew, and validate their passes online, making public transport smoother and more efficient.

Our Vision

Our mission is to revolutionize the way people interact with public transport by embracing digital solutions. With a city like Pune, which sees over 1.5 million people using public transport daily, the need for an efficient, paperless solution has never been more apparent.

By transitioning to digital passes, we aim to eliminate the hassle of standing in long queues, reduce paper waste, and make the whole process more sustainable. With the increasing demand for public transport in urban cities, digital passes will save commuters valuable time and effort, improving the overall transport experience.

Statistics: According to recent data, approximately 1.5 million people in Pune rely on buses for their daily commute. Digital passes can reduce the long wait times, improve accessibility, and increase the overall efficiency of the public transport system.
