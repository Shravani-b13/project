Required Documents

  • Address Proof: Proof of your current address, such as a utility bill or government ID.
  • Passport Size Photos (2): Two recent passport-sized color photographs.
  • Age Proof: Proof of your age, such as leaving certificate

Additional Information

"The Senior Citizen Pass is a special discount pass for individuals aged 60 and above, providing affordable travel options for their daily commute or leisure trips. To apply, ensure your age and address proof documents are up-to-date and valid."
